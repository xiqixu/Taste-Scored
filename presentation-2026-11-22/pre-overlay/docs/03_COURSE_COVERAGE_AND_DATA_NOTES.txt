DESIGN CONSIDERATIONS FROM THE COURSE SLIDE
Overview: introduce vocabulary and the journey before moving into quantitative evidence.
Length: one short summary plus one visible focus statement per stop. Speaker notes remain collapsed; select representative interactions rather than every control.
Form and function: nesting for hierarchy, position for scores, circle area for production, directed links for trade and shared axes for comparisons.
Eyes instead of memory: compare two varieties on one axis and attach the explanation to its current visual mark.
2D and 3D: quantitative views stay flat; the WebGL effect is a transition, not another quantitative dimension.
Immersion: the point-to-fruit animation bridges abstract evidence and physical context; the glass and grain retain the chosen visual atmosphere.
These are design considerations, not mandatory effects or evidence that the assignment requires 3D.

DATA FACTS AND INTERPRETATION
Flavor wheel: SCA/WCR-derived vocabulary via BeanMap, organised into a parent-child JSON tree with equal weight for each terminal term. D3 hierarchy/partition and SVG arcs form the rings. Sector sizes are not sampled flavor frequencies.
CQI: 207 prepared Arabica lots, 22 origin labels, from a May 2023 public scrape. Median total 83.75, range 78–89.33. Taiwan contributes 61 lots (29.5%); 202 have altitude; all sweetness values are 10. The scrape date is not a common harvest year, and the tested sample is not representative of global production.
Processing groups: Washed 127; Natural 46; Honey / pulped natural 26; Experimental 3; Not recorded 5.
Production: 5,127 reporting-area/year records across 85 areas, 1961–2024, green coffee in tonnes. Select FAOSTAT item 656 and element 5510; exclude aggregates from mapped records; preserve source area IDs and flags; compare with OWID values. The national scope is broader than the Arabica lot sample.
2024 denominator: mapped 78 entries sum to 11,133,143.04 tonnes. The separate FAOSTAT world total is 11,284,877.41 tonnes. Brazil plus Vietnam total 5,403,013 tonnes: approximately 47.9% of the world total, or 48.5% of the mapped sum. The concise approved narration does not use this share; retain these denominators if discussing the statistic in Q&A.
Production geography: national reference locations; U.S. point over Hawaii; historic Ethiopia reporting placed at modern Ethiopia's symbol. Small locator rings identify low-volume producers and do not encode another production quantity.
Trade: 106,684 annual exporter/importer records across 230 reporting areas, 1995–2024. Combine HS 090111 and 090112 from CEPII BACI HS92 v202601. Omit absent/zero quantities and specified unmapped reporting groups. Display at most eight largest routes within the current year/filter. Connections are not shipping paths; re-exports can appear in multiple records; imports do not directly measure final consumption.
Variety comparison: Bourbon 17, Caturra 27, Typica 23, Gesha 27, Castillo 1. Relationship diagrams are selected educational links, not complete phylogenies. Unequal counts and differing origin/processing prevent causal ranking.
Matching: six equal-weight quality-score dimensions and Euclidean distance, selecting five nearby CQI lots. It is not a validated model of personal liking or a sensory-intensity scale.
External notes: Firstbloom or named roaster coffees from the same origin label. They describe another coffee, independently of production year and of the matched CQI batch.
Altitude: 202 valid lots, five missing altitude values, Pearson r = 0.17559112175741268. D3 plots position; JavaScript calculates the least-squares line and correlation. This is a weak association, not a causal result.
Cherry: the highest recorded lot is Colombian Castillo, 89.33. CSS artwork and the Web Animations API carry the point into an illustrative fruit. Three.js is not used to build this original fruit sequence.
Processing illustration: reference-based method labels and fruit-layer states, shared HTML/CSS cutaway and JavaScript controls, not an estimated scoring effect.
