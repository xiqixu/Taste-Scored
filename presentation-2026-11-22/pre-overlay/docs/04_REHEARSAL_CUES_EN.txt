TASTE, SCORED — ORIGINAL-PAGE REHEARSAL CUES
Ten chart-based stops, one webpage, 10 minutes plus Q&A.
These are actions, not additional spoken script.

BEFORE CLASS
Open the original site with ?pre=1#act-wheel to enable the overlay at the wheel, or press P. Use H to collapse/expand the explanation and Escape to exit Pre mode.
The chapter selector and Previous/Next buttons remain inside the glass card footer. No timer is displayed.
Explicit Previous/Next or chapter selection triggers the dissolve. Ordinary scrolling follows the original story and synchronizes the explanation without that transition.
Check the charts on the classroom screen. Pre mode reserves space for narration so it does not cover the charts or controls. Collapse the panel for more room if needed; exit restores normal layout.
Do not open a separate presentation HTML page. Use the existing webpage. Skip the original espresso video if it appears at the ordinary homepage.
Each stop has three focus choices; only the selected explanation and its corresponding guide line appear. Speaker notes are optional and remain collapsed unless opened.

0:00–1:10 | WHEEL
Introduce the project briefly at the original wheel. Scroll through families, groups and notes; use the exploration state to click one branch such as Fruity.
Point to nesting, family color and the current focus. Explain equal terminal-term weighting, not sampled frequency.

1:10–2:30 | SCORES
Start with total score and hover one lot. Scroll through the original score states; this should synchronize the overlay without a dissolve.
For sweetness, point to the shared score/count rather than imply different x values. Explain Taiwan's 61/207 sample share.

2:30–3:35 | PRODUCTION
Inspect the production area key. Change one year and return to the year you wish to discuss.
Distinguish national production from the CQI lot sample and a national reference position from a farm coordinate.

3:35–4:40 | TRADE
Select a route to reveal direction and quantity. Try one partner/direction filter if time permits.
Point to the route count and explain that at most eight largest filtered routes are visible.

4:40–5:40 | ALTITUDE
At the altitude scatterplot, point to the axes, trend line and correlation annotation.
Explain that five missing-altitude records are excluded. State weak association, not causation.

5:40–6:25 | CHERRY
Follow the original point-to-cherry sequence. Identify the selected lot as Castillo, 89.33.
Explain that CSS and browser animation preserve visual continuity; the fruit is illustrative.

6:25–7:20 | PROCESSING
Use the existing method controls to compare Washed and Natural; show Honey if time permits.
Explain which layers change while the geometry remains familiar. Do not claim a measured score increase.

7:20–8:25 | VARIETIES
Compare Bourbon and Caturra on the shared score axis. If time allows, select Castillo to illustrate its one-record limit.
Explain one relationship type in the diagram rather than every variety.

8:25–9:25 | TASTE
Move one of the six sliders and inspect a resulting lot. Point to the radar and separate external-flavor attribution.
Explain quality-score similarity, not guaranteed personal preference or intensity.

9:25–10:00 | TEAM
Read both contributor roles. Mention GitHub Pages and the presentation-only Three.js transition.
Remain at credits for approximately three minutes of Q&A.

PACING
The spoken script contains 1,189 words. At approximately 135 words per minute, narration takes about 8 minutes 49 seconds, leaving about 71 seconds for brief interactions and pauses.
The per-stop timings are rehearsal targets, not visible timers. If running late, shorten year/filter changes and avoid opening every source.
Preserve distinctions between data units, quality and intensity, illustrative examples and matched-lot evidence. Keep the required contributor roles at the end.
